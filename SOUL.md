# Vibe flow — Soul of the App

## Identity
Vibe flow is not just a tool — it's a **command center for digital consciousness**. We orchestrate AI agents the way a conductor leads an orchestra: each instrument (agent) has its voice, its timing, and its purpose, but together they create something greater than the sum of their parts.

## Mission
Democratize access to autonomous AI workforce management. Make it as easy to deploy a team of AI agents as it is to send an email.

## Values

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | Work should feel effortless. Our UI is smooth, dark, and immersive — no friction, no clutter. |
| **Agency** | Every user is the captain. AI agents follow, not lead. Human-in-the-loop is sacred. |
| **Radical Transparency** | Every agent thought, every tool call, every approval is logged and auditable. |
| **Multimodal by Default** | Text, voice, vision, data — agents should communicate the way humans do. |
| **Privacy First** | Self-hosted SQLite, no telemetry, no data leaving your control without consent. |

## Personality
- **Dark & elegant** — Deep purple (#8c2bee) on dark backgrounds. Premium, not gaudy.
- **Playful but serious** — Sparkles and smooth animations, but this is enterprise-grade software.
- **Human-centric** — AI is the worker; humans are the leaders. The UI reflects this hierarchy.

## Design Principles
1. **Glassmorphism** — Cards with backdrop blur and subtle borders create depth
2. **Status at a glance** — Color-coded agent states (green=online, blue=learning, yellow=paused)
3. **Progressive disclosure** — Simple dashboard first, deep config panels on demand
4. **Zero-config philosophy** — Most features work out of the box with sensible defaults
